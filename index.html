Laboratory Notes: How Named Entity Recognition Works

1. Preprocessing & Tokenization

Text is cleaned and segmented into individual sentence and word tokens, tracking character start and end offsets to preserve context alignment.

2. Pattern Matching & Gazettes

Rule-based regular expressions (titles like Dr./Prof., standard year/date formats) combined with curated domain dictionaries (cities, organizations, technologies) pinpoint candidate mentions.

3. Disambiguation & Span Resolution

Overlapping spans are prioritized by maximum token length (e.g. multi-word "Apollo Hospital" over individual words), ensuring clean non-conflicting annotations.

4. Classification & Output

Each resolved entity is tagged with its semantic class (PERSON, LOCATION, ORGANIZATION, DATE, TECHNOLOGY) and structured for downstream NLP tasks.
